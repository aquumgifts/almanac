A birth flower gift works so well for moms because it's personal without being complicated. You don't need to guess her taste in jewelry or her size: you need her birth month, and one sentence about what her flower means.

Why moms love birth flower gifts

  • It shows you know her. Her month, her flower, her meaning. Most people have never been told what their birth flower is.
  • It comes with a story. She'll repeat the meaning when she shows it to someone, and that's the part that sticks.
  • It works for any budget. A card, a mug or a framed print can all carry the same meaning.

Her flower, and what it says about her

  • January · Carnation: love that doesn't give up. Carnations have been the Mother's Day flower since the holiday began: its founder, Anna Jarvis, handed out white ones at the first celebration.
  • February · Violet: quiet loyalty.
  • March · Daffodil: new beginnings, for a mom starting a new chapter.
  • April · Daisy: uncomplicated joy.
  • May · Lily of the Valley: happiness returns.
  • June · Rose: love in all its forms. Pink says gratitude.
  • July · Larkspur: an open heart.
  • August · Gladiolus: strength of character, for the mom who held everything together.
  • September · Aster: wisdom and patience.
  • October · Marigold: warmth that stays.
  • November · Chrysanthemum: honest friendship.
  • December · Paperwhite: good wishes for what's ahead.

Each flower has its own guide: see the complete month-by-month guide.

Gift ideas that last longer than a bouquet

  • Something she'll use every day: a mug for her morning coffee, a tote for errands, a soft shirt for weekends.
  • A family flower set: one item with her flower, and her children's flowers alongside it, is a keepsake grandmothers especially love.
  • Seeds or bulbs of her flower, if she gardens: a gift that blooms again every year.
  • A card with her flower's meaning, in your handwriting. Don't skip it: it's what makes the gift hers.

For Mother's Day or her birthday?

Both work, but the birthday is stronger: her birth flower is about her, not about her being your mom. For Mother's Day, the carnation is the traditional choice no matter her month.
