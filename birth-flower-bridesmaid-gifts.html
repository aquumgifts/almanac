The hardest part of bridesmaid gifts is making them match without making them identical. A birth flower solves it: every bridesmaid gets the same style of gift with her own flower. It looks like a set in the photos, and each one is only hers.

Why birth flowers work for a bridal party

  • Matching, but personal. Same design, different flower for each woman.
  • Easy to plan. You only need each bridesmaid's birth month.
  • A meaning to share. Every flower comes with a line you can put in her note.
  • They'll actually use it. A soft shirt for getting ready, a tote for the weekend, a mug for the morning after.

Ideas for the wedding day

  • Getting-ready shirts with each bridesmaid's flower, for the photos everyone takes in the morning.
  • Totes to carry shoes, makeup and emergency kits, one flower each.
  • Mugs for the coffee before hair and makeup.
  • Your own flower in the bouquet, and theirs on the gifts: a detail guests notice.

What to write in her note

Keep it to three lines:

"Your flower is the larkspur. It means an open heart. Thank you for standing next to me."

The month-by-month guide has every flower and its meaning, and our printable chart is handy for planning the whole party at once.

Flowers for the bride

  • May · Lily of the Valley: happiness returns. A classic royal bridal bouquet flower.
  • June · Rose: love in all its forms. The June wedding flower.
  • June · Honeysuckle: devoted affection, June's second flower.
  • September · Morning glory: a love that renews itself every day.

When to order

Made-to-order gifts take about a week to be made and shipped in the US. Order at least three weeks before the wedding or the bridal shower, so there's time to fix a size or a month if needed.
