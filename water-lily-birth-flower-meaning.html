Water Lily Birth Flower: Meaning, History and July Gift Ideas

The water lily is July's second birth flower, next to the larkspur. It floats on still water among round green pads, opening its pointed petals in the morning sun and closing them again in the evening.

What the water lily means

The water lily stands for purity and rebirth.

  • Rebirth. Many water lilies close at night and open again each morning, a daily renewal that made them symbols of new life.
  • Purity. The flower rises clean and bright from muddy water.
  • Peace and calm. Few things look as serene as a pond of water lilies in summer.
A water lily says: "Every day is a chance to begin again."

A little history

  • Ancient Egypt. The "blue lotus" of ancient Egyptian art is actually a water lily, Nymphaea caerulea. Because it opened with the sunrise, it was linked to the sun and to rebirth.
  • Monet. Claude Monet painted the water lilies in his garden pond at Giverny around 250 times, some of the most famous paintings in the world.
  • Not the lotus. The sacred lotus of Asia is a different plant, with its flowers held high above the water. Water lilies float on the surface.

Water lily or larkspur?

  • The larkspur means an open heart: tall, colorful and full of summer energy. Read the larkspur guide.
  • The water lily is calmer and more reflective. It suits someone serene, someone starting a new chapter, or someone who loves art and water.
