A gift tag is the first thing someone reads on Christmas morning. These ones say "Merry Christmas" with the birth flower of the person the gift is for, what the flower means, a sprig of holly and lines for To and From.

Download the free Christmas gift tags (PDF)

One letter-size page with twelve tags, one for every month. Print one page per person you're wrapping for, or a few to have spares.

How to make them

  1. Print on cardstock (65–110 lb) at 100% or "actual size". Cream or kraft-colored cardstock looks lovely with the holly.
  2. Cut along the dashed lines.
  3. Punch the small circle at the top and tie with ribbon, twine or a strand of red and white baker's twine.

Why a birth flower tag

On a pile of presents, every tag looks the same. A tag with their flower tells them the gift was chosen for them before they even open it, and it gives the kids something to look for under the tree.
