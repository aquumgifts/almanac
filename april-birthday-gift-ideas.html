April Birthday Gift Ideas: Uncomplicated Joy, Starting With the Daisy

April birthdays come with spring in full swing and, some years, Easter in the same week. It's an easy month to celebrate, and the right gift should feel just as light.

The fix is a gift that's clearly about them, and the easiest way to get there is their month's flower.

Start with April's birth flower

April's birth flower is the daisy, and it stands for innocence and uncomplicated joy. It's one of the first flowers children learn to draw, and it keeps that sense of lightness. April's second flower, the sweet pea, means blissful pleasure, and in the Victorian language of flowers it also meant "thank you for a lovely time."

Read the full April birth flower guide.

April birthday gift ideas

  • A daisy shirt, mug or tote for someone who makes everything feel easier.
  • A picnic plan: April is the first month that really invites one.
  • Sweet pea seeds to plant together; they climb and bloom by early summer.
  • A card with the meaning: "The daisy means uncomplicated joy. That's what you bring."

Before you give it

If Easter falls the same week, give the birthday gift on the birthday itself, without bunnies or eggs on the wrapping.

Order early

Made-to-order gifts take about a week to be made and shipped in the US, so order at least a week before the birthday.
