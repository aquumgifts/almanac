September Birthday Gift Ideas: Wisdom and Patience, With the Aster

2026-10-01

Written by the team behind Aquum, a small birth flower gift shop. How we make money.

September birthdays arrive with the back-to-school rush: new schedules, new teachers, everyone busy. The birthday gets squeezed between school supplies and the first cool week.

The fix is a gift that's clearly about them, and the easiest way to get there is their month's flower.

Start with September's birth flower

September's birth flower is the aster, and it stands for wisdom and patience. Its star-shaped flowers bloom as summer turns to fall. September's second flower, the morning glory, means affection: it opens every morning, a love that renews itself every day.

September birthday gift ideas

  • An aster shirt, mug or tote for the calm one in every group.
  • A teacher's birthday: the aster is the teacher flower. See our gifts for teachers.
  • Morning glory seeds for someone who loves a fresh start every day.
  • A slow weekend plan, after the first busy weeks of school.

Before you give it

Mind the calendar. If school starts the same week, celebrate the weekend after, when everyone can breathe.

Order early

Made-to-order gifts take about a week to be made and shipped in the US, so order at least a week before the birthday.
