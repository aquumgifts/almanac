About The Birth Flower Almanac

The Birth Flower Almanac is a small guide to the flowers of each month: what they mean, where those meanings come from, and how to turn them into a gift that says something.

It's written by the team behind Aquum, a small shop that makes botanical birth flower tees. When we mention Aquum, that's us, and we'll always say so.

Meanings come from long-standing traditions in the language of flowers, and they vary by culture. Where a flower carries a very different meaning somewhere else, we point it out, so your gift lands the way you mean it.

Free to use

Everything here is free to read, and some of it is free to print: the birth flower chart with all twelve months on one page, and a sheet of birth flower gift tags. If you only need one flower, the birth flower finder gives you the flower and its meaning for any birthday.

We've also made a set of printable birthday cards, one for every month.
