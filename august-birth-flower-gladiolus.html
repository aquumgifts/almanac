August Birth Flower: Gladiolus Meaning and Gift Ideas

2026-09-30

August's birth flowers are the gladiolus and the poppy. The gladiolus is tall and striking, with flowers opening one after another up a single stem.

What the gladiolus means

The gladiolus stands for strength of character and integrity. Its name comes from the Latin gladius, sword, because of the shape of its leaves. In ancient Rome it was linked to gladiators, and it has kept that sense of courage.

The poppy, August's second flower

The poppy means imagination and remembrance. The red poppy in particular is worn to honor soldiers who died in war, so it carries real weight.

Who the gladiolus suits

  • Someone born in August
  • Nurses, caregivers and anyone who looks after others for a living
  • The person who held the family together through a hard year
A gladiolus says: I see how strong you've been.

August birth flower gift ideas

  • A gladiolus shirt, mug or tote, a daily reminder of their strength.
  • Gladiolus bulbs to plant in spring, for tall blooms at the end of summer.
  • A card with its meaning, for someone who never hears it said out loud.
