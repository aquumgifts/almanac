Sweet Pea Birth Flower: Meaning, History and April Gift Ideas

The sweet pea is April's second birth flower, next to the daisy. It's a climbing flower with ruffled petals that look like little butterflies, curling tendrils, and one of the loveliest scents in any garden.

What the sweet pea means

The sweet pea stands for blissful pleasure.

  • Blissful pleasure. Light, fragrant and pretty, it's a flower about simple delight.
  • Thank you, and goodbye. In Victorian flower language, sweet peas were given to say "thank you for a lovely time", often at the end of a visit. That makes them a gentle farewell or thank-you flower.
  • Kindness and gratitude in general, which suits April birthdays and teachers alike.
A sweet pea says: "Thank you for every good moment with you."

A little history

  • From Sicily. Wild sweet peas grow in Sicily. A monk named Francisco Cupani is credited with sending seeds to England around 1699.
  • The Victorian favorite. Breeders in the 1800s, especially Henry Eckford, created hundreds of varieties in every color, and sweet peas became a symbol of the era.
  • Not for eating. Unlike garden peas, sweet pea seeds and pods are toxic if eaten. Enjoy them in a vase.

Sweet pea or daisy?

  • The daisy means uncomplicated joy and innocence: bright, simple and cheerful. Read the daisy guide.
  • The sweet pea is softer and more romantic, and its "thank you" meaning makes it perfect for someone you're grateful to.

April birthday gift ideas with the sweet pea

  • A packet of sweet pea seeds and some string to climb on: they're sown in early spring and bloom all summer.
  • A sweet pea design on something they use every day: a mug, a tote, a shirt.
  • A thank-you gift for someone born in April, a teacher or a mentor. See our teacher gift guide.
  • Daisy and sweet pea together, both April flowers.
