August Birthday Gift Ideas: Strength of Character, in a Flower

August is the last stretch of summer, when people are traveling, moving or getting ready for school to start. Birthdays this month often get a quick text from the beach.

The fix is a gift that's clearly about them, and the easiest way to get there is their month's flower.

Start with August's birth flower

August's birth flower is the gladiolus, and it stands for strength of character and integrity. Its name comes from the Latin for sword, after the shape of its leaves. August's second flower, the poppy, means imagination and remembrance.

August birthday gift ideas

  • A gladiolus shirt, mug or tote for someone who held it all together this year.
  • A thank-you birthday: August birthdays are perfect for nurses, caregivers and the people who never stop. See our gifts for nurses.
  • A poppy print for a creative friend, for imagination.
  • A plan before the season ends: one last summer evening, on the day.

Before you give it

If they're traveling on the day, send the gift ahead, or plan a small celebration the week they get back.
