A bookmark with their birth flower is one of the smallest gifts you can give, and one of the most used. Each one has the month, the flower and what it means, with a little hole at the top for a ribbon or tassel.

Download the free birth flower bookmarks (PDF)

Three letter-size pages, four bookmarks per page, one for every month. Each bookmark is 2 × 7 inches.

How to make them

  1. Print on cardstock (80–110 lb) at 100% or "actual size".
  2. Cut along the dashed lines.
  3. Punch the small circle at the top and tie a ribbon or a tassel through it.
  4. Optional: laminate them, or cover with clear packing tape, so they last for years.

Ideas for using them

  • Tucked into a book you give: a birthday book with their flower inside feels personal without costing more.
  • For a book club or a classroom: print a set and let everyone take their own month. Teachers, see our teacher gift guide.
  • Party favors: a bookmark for each guest at a baby shower or a birthday party. More ideas in our baby shower guide.
  • With a card: pair a bookmark with one of our free birthday cards.
