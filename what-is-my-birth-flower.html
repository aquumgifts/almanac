Why do some months have two flowers?

Birth flower lists come from old European and Victorian traditions, and different sources settled on different flowers. Most modern lists agree on the main flower for each month and add a second one. You can use either; our guide to second birth flowers explains when the second one fits better.

Is it the same as a birthstone?

It works the same way: one per month, each with its own meaning. The difference is that flowers are softer and more personal as a gift, and much easier to put on everyday things like a shirt, a mug or a card.
