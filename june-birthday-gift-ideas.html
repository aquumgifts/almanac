June birthdays compete with weddings, graduations and the start of summer. The good news: June's birth flower is the one everyone already loves.

The fix is a gift that's clearly about them, and the easiest way to get there is their month's flower.

Start with June's birth flower

June's birth flower is the rose, and it stands for love in all its forms. The color decides which form: red for romance, pink for gratitude and admiration, yellow for friendship and joy, white for respect and new beginnings. June's second flower, the honeysuckle, means devoted affection.

June birthday gift ideas

  • A rose shirt, mug or tote, with a note about the color you chose and why.
  • A single garden rose in the color that fits your relationship, with the gift.
  • Honeysuckle for a garden fence, if they grow things.
  • A summer evening plan, outdoors, on the actual day.

Before you give it

Choose the color on purpose. A yellow rose for a best friend says something a red one never could. More in our guide to birth flower color meanings.

Order early

Made-to-order gifts take about a week to be made and shipped in the US, so order at least a week before the birthday.
