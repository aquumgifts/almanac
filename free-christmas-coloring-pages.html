Free Christmas Coloring Pages with Birth Flowers

A quiet activity for the week before Christmas: each page says "Merry Christmas" in outline letters, with a birth flower to color and two sprigs of holly. Every child gets the page with their own birth month, so the tree of finished drawings ends up being a little portrait of the family.

Download the free Christmas coloring pages (PDF)

Twelve letter-size pages, one for each month: January's carnation through December's paperwhite. Bold, simple lines that work for younger kids, with enough detail in the petals for older ones.

Ideas for using them

  • Homemade Christmas cards: color a page, fold it in half and write inside. Grandparents keep these. Need words? Here's what to write in a Christmas card.
  • Kids' table at Christmas dinner: a page and a few crayons at each child's seat, with their name on our place cards.
  • Classroom party: print one page per student by birth month. Teachers, see our teacher gift guide.
  • Gift wrap: colored pages make lovely wrapping for small, flat presents. Add a Christmas gift tag.
  • An advent activity: one page a day for the last twelve days before Christmas.

Printing tips

  • Regular printer paper is fine for crayons and colored pencils; use thicker paper (65 lb cardstock) for markers or watercolor.
  • Print at 100% or "actual size".
  • For the classic look, color the holly berries red and the leaves deep green, and leave the flower in its real colors: pink carnation, purple violet, yellow daffodil and so on.
