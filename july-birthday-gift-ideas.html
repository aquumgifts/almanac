July Birthday Gift Ideas: An Open Heart, Not Fireworks

July birthdays happen in the middle of summer: vacations, Fourth of July cookouts, friends out of town. It's easy for the day to blur into the season.

The fix is a gift that's clearly about them, and the easiest way to get there is their month's flower.

Start with July's birth flower

July's birth flower is the larkspur, and it stands for an open heart and a strong bond. It grows in tall spikes of blue, purple, pink or white. July's second flower, the water lily, means purity and rebirth: it opens in the morning and rises clean from muddy water.

July birthday gift ideas

  • A larkspur shirt, mug or tote for the friend who stopped being "just a friend" years ago.
  • A plan for when everyone is back: a birthday dinner in late July or August, so people can actually come.
  • A water lily print for someone starting over this year.
  • A card with the meaning: "The larkspur means an open heart. Thank you for yours."

Before you give it

A safety note: larkspur is toxic to people, pets and livestock if eaten. As a cut flower out of reach it's fine; as a plant for a home with curious pets or small children, a design is the worry-free version.

Order early

Made-to-order gifts take about a week to be made and shipped in the US, so order at least a week before the birthday.
