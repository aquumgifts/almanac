Three letter-size pages, four cards per page, one card for every month. Each card is 4.25 × 5.5 inches, the size of a standard A2 envelope.

How to make them

  1. Print on white or cream cardstock (65–110 lb), at 100% or "actual size".
  2. Cut along the dashed lines.
  3. Write your message on the back, or glue the card onto a folded blank card for a sturdier one.

Why a birth flower Christmas card

Most Christmas cards could go to anyone. A card with their own flower shows you thought about the person, not only the season. A few ideas:

  • For family far away: one card per person in the house, each with their flower.
  • Tucked into a gift: a birth flower card with a small present says more than a tag. See our Christmas gift guide.
  • For December birthdays: someone born in December gets a lot of combined "birthday and Christmas" gifts. Give them a separate birthday card too, and they'll notice.
