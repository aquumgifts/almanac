A place card with each guest's birth flower turns a holiday table into a small surprise. Everyone finds their seat, and their flower, and someone always asks "what does mine mean?". It's an easy conversation starter for Thanksgiving, Christmas dinner, a wedding or a shower.

Download the free birth flower place cards (PDF)

Two letter-size pages, six cards per page, one for every month. Each card folds into a tent 3.5 inches wide and 2 inches tall, and shows the flower on both sides.

How to make them

  1. Print on cardstock (65–110 lb) at 100% or "actual size".
  2. Cut along the dashed lines.
  3. Fold each card along the dotted line in the middle, so it stands up like a tent.
  4. Write each guest's name on the line, on both sides.

Print as many pages as you need: if three guests were born in June, print the page with June three times.

Make it a little game

Before dinner, ask everyone to guess what their flower means, then read the meanings out loud. The birth flowers by month guide has each one, and the finder helps if you don't know a guest's birthday month by heart.
