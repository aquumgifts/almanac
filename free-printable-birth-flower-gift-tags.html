One letter-size page with twelve tags, one for every month. Print it on cardstock, cut along the dashed lines, punch the small circle and tie the tag on with ribbon or twine.

How to use them

  • Match the tag to the person. Find their month and use their flower. Not sure? Type their birthday into the birth flower finder.
  • Add one line on the back. "Your flower means love that doesn't give up. So do you."
  • For Christmas, a tag with each person's own flower turns a pile of presents into something personal. More ideas in our Christmas gift guide.
  • For birthdays, it's the finishing touch on any gift, even a store-bought one.

Printing tips

  • Cardstock (65–110 lb) makes the tags sturdy; regular paper works if you glue it to kraft paper.
  • Print at 100% scale, not "fit to page," so the tags stay the right size.
  • Use it for your own gifts as often as you like. Please don't resell the file.
