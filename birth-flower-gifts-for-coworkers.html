Office gifts have one rule: personal enough to feel thoughtful, neutral enough to feel comfortable. A birth flower sits right in that space. Nobody is embarrassed by a flower, and it shows you paid attention to their birthday.

Why it works at work

  • It's about their month, not about their personal life.
  • The meaning gives you something to say without a long speech.
  • It's easy to keep under budget. A card, a small print or a shirt all land well under $30.

Ideas by relationship

  • A teammate: a card with their flower and its meaning. Download our free birth flower birthday cards.

  • A mentor: the flower plus one line of thanks. September's aster means wisdom and patience, which fits.
  • A team gift: one flower for each person on the team, in a single frame or on a bulletin board.
