The birthdays that get lost

Four months have it harder than the rest, because the birthday lands next to a holiday:

  • November: folded into Thanksgiving. See November birthday ideas.
  • December: swallowed by Christmas. See December birthday ideas.
  • January: right after the holidays, when everyone is done with gifts. See January birthday ideas.
  • October: buried under Halloween decorations. See October birthday ideas.

For these, a gift with their own flower does double duty: it's personal, and it's clearly about them, not the season.

Three rules for any birthday gift

  • Make it about them, not about the date on the calendar.
  • Put the meaning in the card. One line about their flower, one line about them.
  • Order a week ahead for anything made to order.
